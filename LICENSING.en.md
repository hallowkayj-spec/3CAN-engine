> The unmodified terms in [LICENSE](./LICENSE) control. This FAQ is a plain-language project explanation, not legal advice.

## Which license applies?

**source-available** license, not an OSI-approved open-source license.

## What is generally permitted?

Subject to `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`, permitted uses generally include:

- personal study, research, experiments, testing, and hobby projects with no anticipated commercial application;
- use by the noncommercial organizations identified in the license;
- modification and creation of new works for those permitted purposes;
- distribution of copies or modified versions with the license terms and Required Notices; and
- issues, discussions, and pull-request contributions.

## What requires separate permission?

Any commercial use requires separate written permission from the licensor. Examples include:

- internal use by a company or other commercial organization;
- client work, deliverables, paid consulting, or hosted services;
- SaaS, paid products, commercial plugins, or revenue-generating services; and
- work with an anticipated commercial application, even if it is not charging users yet.

No direct fee does not automatically make a use noncommercial. Contact the maintainer before use if the classification is unclear.

## May I fork, modify, or redistribute it?

Yes, for a permitted noncommercial purpose. Distribution must include these license terms or their URL and every applicable `Required Notice:` line from `NOTICE`.

## May I use it at work?

Company-internal use is a commercial scenario for this project's licensing policy. Do not use 3CAN in internal systems, client work, or commercial products without separate written permission.

## Why not Apache-2.0, MIT, or GPL?

Those OSI-approved licenses allow commercial use and therefore do not implement the project's “commercial use requires authorization” policy. PolyForm Noncommercial provides a standard noncommercial boundary while leaving separate commercial licensing available.

## How are contributions licensed?

Ordinary contributions are offered under the repository's PolyForm Noncommercial terms. If a contribution is to be included in a separately licensed commercial version, the maintainer will request an explicit, lightweight contributor agreement before merge; no additional rights are assumed silently.

## How do I request commercial permission?

Contact `hallowkayj-spec` through the GitHub profile or this repository's discussion/issue channel. Ask only to establish private contact; do not post confidential commercial details publicly.

## Is this legal advice?

No. The `LICENSE` text controls, and important use cases should be reviewed by qualified counsel.
